Audio Monitor Stands

Year
Summer 2024
Duration
1 Day
Status
Complete

The Motive

I wanted a clean, simple solution to get my speakers off of my desk. Instead of paying an exorbitant amount for basic stands, I decided to make my own.

The Solution

To begin the design, I approximately measured the vertical distance from my ears to the surface of my desk. This is the height I wanted to place the center of the subwoofers.

The stand was designed in two halves to reduce the support material required when printing. The two pieces fit together without requiring any fasteners.

Technology Stack

Autodesk CAD
